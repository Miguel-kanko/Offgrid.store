# offgrid.store - Offline Marketplace for Awaé
> Commerce without internet for rural Cameroon.

**Author:** KANKO DJOUA MIGUEL
**Status:** L1, Institut Africain d'Informatique (IAI) - Yaoundé, Cameroon
**Goal:** Master's in Computer Science - Research on offline-first systems
**Location:** Awaé, Yaoundé

### The Problem
Vendors in Awaé have no stable internet. They cannot sell on Jumia.

### The Solution
An offline-first mobile app that works 100% offline. Built with Flutter + SQLite.

### Products Database
10 local products included: Telephone, School bags, Television set, Laptop, Cars, Motobikes, Pots, Shoes, Clothes, Books

### Future
Deploy in Awaé markets after my Master's degree. Return to Cameroon to develop local tech.
